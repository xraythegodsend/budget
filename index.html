Each paycheck

Each card is that week's column from the Bills page. Tick a row as you move the money; hover any row or jar for what's been paid against that bill this month. A row marked ↗ also feeds a goal or a debt — ticking it moves that number too.

When bills are due

Every bill on the day it actually lands. Today is ringed.

Balance runway

Money coming in

A column per paycheck, same as the bills below. Amount and How often set the suggested split; type into any week to override it — a bonus week, or a paycheck that lands light. Untick a source to take it out of every total.

Bills

One column per paycheck. Total needed is what one round of the bill costs, and Due is when it's due — a weekday for a weekly bill, a real date for anything else. Neither has anything to do with when you get paid. The week cells are yours: type what you'll take out that week, and the Row total tells you whether it adds up to what the bill needs this month. Leave a cell alone and it uses a suggested amount; ↺ puts a row back to suggested.

What you're saving for

One row per pot you're filling. Target is the number you're heading for and Saved is where you've got to; Ready by works out when the two meet at the monthly amount. Funded by is the useful one: point a goal at a bill, and every time you tick that bill off on This month, what you moved lands in this pot.

What you're paying off

One row per debt. Started at is the balance you began tracking from — it's only there to measure progress against. Owed now is the number you keep up to date, and Paid by keeps it up to date for you: name the bill that pays this debt, and ticking that bill off on This month takes the payment straight off the balance. Untick it and the balance goes back up by exactly what it went down. Clear by works out how long the rest takes at the monthly payment, allowing for the rate.

I have a saved file instead

If you saved your budget with Save data file, it's a file called budget-data.js. On a computer it's usually in Downloads. On an iPhone, an emailed file needs Share → Save to Files first, then find it under Browse.
